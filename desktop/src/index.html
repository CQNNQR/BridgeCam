    ◉ Motion Detection
    How It Works Motion detection compares consecutive video frames by computing the mean absolute luma difference across a 160×90 downsampled grid at 5 fps. When the difference exceeds the sensitivity threshold, recording starts automatically. Recording continues as long as motion is detected and stops 5 seconds after the last detected movement.
    Default Sensitivity 1 = subtle movement only, 100 = very sensitive (1–100)
    Auto-Enable on Connect Automatically start motion detection when phone connects
